# Editing App SEO growth plan

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Positioning after competitor review

The closest broad product competitor is [VEED](https://www.veed.io/), because it combines browser editing and AI media generation. The closest specialist competitors are [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/features) for product-URL performance ads and [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/) for long-video repurposing. [Runway](https://runwayml.com/pricing) is the strongest benchmark for multi-model generation and credit communication.

Editing App should not try to rank for the generic promise “best AI video generator” alone. Its defensible search position is:

- one private workspace for editing, generation, product ads, image creation, and cutouts;
- explicit model selection plus capability-aware Autopilot;
- honest duration and resolution limits per model;
- durable background jobs and paid-result delivery checkpoints;
- source-aware performance creative that avoids unsupported product claims.

## On-page implementation

- Unique crawlable pages exist for all five product capabilities under `/features/`.
- Every feature page has a unique title, description, canonical URL, H1, workflow, benefits, FAQ copy, and structured data.
- Root metadata, Open Graph image, Twitter metadata, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` are generated by Next.js metadata files.
- Private application and API routes are excluded from crawling.
- Homepage cards link to public feature pages instead of sending every visitor directly to sign-up.

## Off-page program

Off-page SEO requires publishing or outreach through accounts owned by the business. Do not automate external submissions without explicit authorization. Execute in this order:

1. Create complete product profiles on Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, G2, Capterra, and relevant AI tool directories. Link to the most relevant feature page, not only the homepage.
2. Publish a quarterly “AI video model capability index” comparing verified duration, native audio, resolution, price, and retry behavior. This should become the primary linkable asset.
3. Ask fal.ai model partners, ecommerce agencies, and creator educators for integration-directory or workflow-guide links. Offer a tested model matrix or co-authored tutorial instead of reciprocal link swaps.
4. Publish source-backed comparisons for high-intent searches: “Editing App vs VEED,” “Editing App vs Creatify,” and “Editing App vs OpusClip.” Keep comparison claims factual and date-stamped.
5. Create workflow case studies with measurable inputs and outputs: source minutes, final clips, generation model, resolution, elapsed time, and credit use.
6. Reclaim unlinked brand mentions and broken links monthly. Avoid paid link networks, bulk guest-post packages, and misleading “best” badges.

## Measurement

Track non-brand clicks and qualified sign-ups by landing page. Priority query clusters:

- AI video editor with model selection
- Seedance 2.5 video generator
- AI product URL to video ad
- long video to short-form content
- private AI video generator
- AI image generator with Seedream and Recraft
- background remover for product photos

Review search impressions, click-through rate, indexed-page health, referring domains, and sign-up conversion monthly. Update model facts whenever provider documentation changes.
