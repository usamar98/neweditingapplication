# Editing App off-page SEO kit

Last reviewed: 2026-08-19

Off-page SEO requires business-owned accounts and human review. Use this kit to earn relevant mentions and links; do not use bulk link networks, automated comment spam, paid link packages, or fake review profiles.

## Canonical destinations

- Homepage: `https://www.editingapp.live/`
- Product URL to video: `https://www.editingapp.live/tools/product-url-to-video`
- Long video to shorts: `https://www.editingapp.live/tools/long-video-to-shorts`
- AI video generator: `https://www.editingapp.live/tools/ai-video-generator`
- Image to video AI: `https://www.editingapp.live/tools/image-to-video-ai`

- Product photo background remover: `https://www.editingapp.live/tools/product-photo-background-remover`
- AI video model guide: `https://www.editingapp.live/ai-video-models`

Link to the page that best answers the referring page's audience. Do not force every mention to the homepage.

The referring website must use a crawlable HTML link such as `<a href="https://www.editingapp.live/tools/ai-video-generator">AI video generator</a>`. Editing App can provide correct canonical and preview metadata, but it cannot force Google to crawl or credit a link on another website.

Editing App is a private creative workspace for editing and generating campaign media. Upload long footage for scene, transcript, silence, highlight, caption, crop, and audio suggestions; generate video with compatibility-aware Seedance, LTX, Veo, and Kling routes; create images with Seedream, Recraft, Nano Banana, and FLUX; turn product URLs into source-aware ads; and remove backgrounds through private, durable jobs. ## Submission order

1. Product Hunt: homepage, original product screenshots, honest launch story, and model-routing differentiator.
2. AlternativeTo: homepage with the browser editor and multi-model workspace category; list real alternatives without attacking them.
3. G2 and Capterra: use the business profile, consistent name, canonical URL, support contact, and only verified customer reviews.
4. AI tool directories: submit only editorially maintained directories that expose a crawlable profile and do not demand a followed backlink in exchange.
5. Ecommerce and agency communities: link to the product-URL workflow only when it directly answers a question or accompanies a tested workflow.

## Linkable assets

- Quarterly AI video model capability index: verified duration, native audio, resolution, ideal use, and source link.
- Product URL ad benchmark: one source page, the same brief, each supported model, measured render time and credit cost.
- Long-video repurposing case study: source minutes, suggested hooks, approved clips, edit time, and delivery formats.
- Private AI media processing explainer: signed links, durable jobs, retry protection, and deletion behavior.

Publish measured results and limitations. A useful benchmark earns more durable links than a generic “best tools” article.

## Measurement

Add campaign tags only to outreach and profile links, never to canonical URLs:

`?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=off_page_launch`

Track monthly:

- new referring domains and their topical relevance;
- clicks and sign-ups by landing page;
- branded versus non-brand Search Console queries;
- links to the model guide and workflow pages;
- lost links, unlinked brand mentions, and directory profile accuracy.
